# Investor Behaviour Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze investor demographics, saving objectives, investment preferences, monitoring habits, and information sources.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of how investors differ by gender and helps identify the investment objectives, avenues, and information sources that are most prominent within the analyzed dataset.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **investor behaviour using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard brings together demographic information and investment-related preferences to provide insights into how investors approach saving and investment decisions.

The analysis covers:

- Investor demographics
- Saving objectives
- Investment objectives
- Investment preferences
- Investment monitoring habits
- Information sources
- Gender-based comparisons

Interactive filters allow users to explore the analysis based on **gender, investment avenue, and investment duration**.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights three key demographic indicators:

- **Male Investors:** 100
- **Female Investors:** 60
- **Average Investor Age:** 27.80 years

---

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains **100 male investors and 60 female investors**, with an average investor age of approximately **27.8 years**.
- **Capital appreciation** is the most common investment objective, with **104 investors**, followed by growth with 44 investors and income with 12 investors.
- **Mutual funds** are the most monitored investment avenue, accounting for **45%** of the monitoring distribution.
- Male investors show greater interest in **retirement planning** compared with female investors in the analyzed data.
- Male investors show higher participation across the major investment preferences analyzed, including **government bonds, equity markets, and mutual funds**.
- **Financial consultants and newspapers/magazines** are prominent information sources among investors, particularly among male investors.
- The dashboard enables users to compare investor behaviour by **gender, investment avenue, and investment duration**.

## Analytical Approach

The project followed a structured business analytics workflow:

1. **Data Preparation**
   - Reviewed investor-related demographic and investment data.
   - Identified relevant variables for behavioural analysis.

2. **Data Transformation**
   - Prepared the data for analysis using Power Query.
   - Organized categorical and numerical fields for visualization.

3. **Data Modeling**
   - Developed the required data model within Power BI.
   - Created analytical calculations and measures using DAX.

4. **Dashboard Development**
   - Designed KPI cards and interactive visualizations.
   - Added slicers for gender, investment avenue, and investment duration.

5. **Analysis**
   - Compared investment behaviour across genders.
   - Identified dominant investment objectives, avenues, monitoring habits, and information sources.
